## What Martin said (and what went wrong before)
"I don't see any of the different starting patterns - it is always just random. Also, add an FPS counter, and look at the pl-box and add different starting rulesets."

My previous brief (conductor error) treated "starting patterns" as only the 31 `RulePreset` rule generators. That is not what he means. pl-box's real starting patterns are:
- **Spawn layouts** in `~/code/pl-box/crates/pl-core/src/simulation.rs`: `spawn_pie`, `spawn_line`, `spawn_ring`, `spawn_rainbow_ring`, `spawn_rainbow_rings`, `spawn_soft_clusters`, `spawn_radiant_fans`, `spawn_spiral`, `spawn_twin_spirals`, `spawn_spiral_arms`, `spawn_blocks`, `spawn_line_mixed`, `spawn_stripes`, `spawn_polar_maze`, `spawn_heart`, `spawn_star`, and the weighted chooser `spawn_pattern` (read the file for the exact list, shapes and weights; `bin/render.rs` shows the names). pl-box's own comment says uniform-random starts leave directional rules as diffuse dust and that structured starts let them organise. This is exactly why about a third of our presets look like gas.
- **Curated presets**: `CURATED` in pl-core (find where it is defined: `grep -rn CURATED crates/pl-core/src`), each bundling its own tuned physics (rule matrix, force scale, friction, core repel, radii ranges, type count) plus `color::curated_palette`. `main.rs` starts from a random curated preset and, on each new preset, respawns the particles with a spawn pattern (see its `install curated preset` macro and the key handling around lines 178-400, including the spawn keys 4-9 and the auto-restart). Read `main.rs` for how pl-box cycles.
